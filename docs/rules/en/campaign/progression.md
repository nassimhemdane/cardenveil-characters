# Campaign advancement

> **Campaign variant — work in progress.** These optional rules are a special case, separate from the core game. They apply only when the GM chooses Campaign mode; their purchases, caps and mastery restrictions do not apply by default in Simple Adventure mode.

## Starting campaign pool

In Campaign mode, the starting pool contains only cards from **2 through 8 inclusive**, in all four suits. Remove cards from **9 through Ace** from the decks at the start. The pool then evolves according to the advancement rules below.

## 🎲 Advancement system

Characters advance by earning **XP**, awarded by the GM for memorable actions, combat or narrative developments.

XP is spent to improve the character, but level depends on the **cumulative total XP acquired**: spending XP never lowers level.

- A character starts at **level 1**.
- **Level = 1 + ⌊XP acquired ÷ 5⌋**.
- Each level gained grants **1 attribute point** to assign to Strength, Agility, Spirit or Social.
- In Campaign mode, an attribute cannot exceed **20**.
- Increasing an attribute causes **all derived statistics to be recalculated**.

**Level** also determines the limits of the system:

- It sets the **advancement cap**: **⌊level ÷ 2⌋**.

## 🎯 Advancement paths

The character advances by spending XP on different improvements, each corresponding to an aspect of play (combat, magic, narrative, resources).

- **Weapons (families)** → 2 XP per level (1 XP when catching up): improves use of a weapon family
- **Unique weapons** → 1 XP per level: improves a specific weapon
- **Suit Mastery** → 4 XP: reduces the cost of abilities of one suit
- **Ability Awakening** → 1 XP: improves one specific ability
- **Elemental Mastery** → 3 XP: strengthens an element
- **Learning an ability** → 2 XP: adds a new ability
- **Skill** → 3 XP: adds a skill
- **Aspect** → 3 XP: adds a narrative aspect
- **HP** → 2 XP: increases hit points
- **Feat** → 5 XP: grants a unique passive
- **Pool advancement** → collective cost of **3 XP × number of players**: improves the card pool

## 🗡️ Weapons (families)

**Families:** straight swords, curved swords, axes, clubs, reach weapons, bows, crossbows, foci, shields

**Levels:**

- Unmastered → damage + Mod, no properties
- Mastered → access to family and weapon properties
- Perfection +N → +N damage and -N cost for **Diamond** abilities associated with the weapon

**Cost:**

- 2 XP per level
- 1 XP if the family is below your current highest tier (catching up)

**Requirement:**

- Training / practice with the family

**Cap:**

- ⌊level ÷ 2⌋

---

## 🗡️ Unique weapons

**Levels:**

- Unlocks the unique property
- Perfection +N → identical scaling (+N damage, -N Diamond cost)

**Cost:**

- 1 XP per level

**Requirement:**

- Acquisition / use

**Cap:**

- ⌊level ÷ 2⌋

---

## 🃏 Suit Mastery

**Effect:**

- -1 cost for all abilities of one suit

**Cost:**

- 4 XP

**Advancement:**

- Stackable

**Requirement:**

- Repeated use
- GM approval

**Cap:**

- ⌊level ÷ 2⌋

---

## ✨ Ability Awakening

**Effect:**

- -1 cost for this ability

**Cost:**

- 1 XP

**Advancement:**

- Stackable on the same ability

**Requirement:**

- 5 **critical uses**, meaning five uses in moments when the ability is genuinely relevant. The GM decides which uses count, preventing artificial repetition performed solely to unlock the Awakening.

**Cap:**

- ⌊level ÷ 2⌋

---

## 🔥 Elemental Mastery

**Effect:**

- +1 to Spirit Mod for abilities of that element

**Cost:**

- 3 XP

**Advancement:**

- Stackable

**Requirement:**

- GM approval (exposure / use)

**Cap:**

- ⌊level ÷ 2⌋

---

## 📘 Abilities

**Effect:**

- Learn a new ability

**Cost:**

- 2 XP

**Requirement:**

- Roleplay justification (mentor, discovery, training)

---

## 📚 Skills

**Effect:**

- +1 proficient skill

**Cost:**

- 3 XP

**Requirement:**

- Learning + practice (roleplay justification)

---

## 🎭 Aspects

**Effect:**

- +1 narrative aspect

**Cost:**

- 3 XP

**Requirement:**

- GM approval

---

## 🛡️ Hit Points

**Effect:**

- +5 HP + 0.5 × Strength

**Cost:**

- 2 XP

**Requirement:**

- None

---

## 🧩 Feats

**Effect:**

- Unique passive

**Cost:**

- 5 XP

**Requirement:**

- Acquired through training / an event (justification)

---

## 🃏 Card pool

**Effect:**

- Change the maximum card in the pool:
    - either add the next card (e.g. 8 → 9 → 10 → … → King)
    - or remove the lowest card

**Cost:**

- **3 XP × number of players**, paid collectively. Contributions may be divided freely: one player may pay more than another. Each XP counts as spent by the character who pays it.

**Requirement:**

- Collective decision
