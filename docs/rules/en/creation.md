# Character

# 📝 **Character Creation**

---

### **1. Identity**

- Choose a **name**, a **race**, an **alignment** and a **short description**.
- Add a few **physical or cultural traits** if you wish.

---

### **2. Attributes**

- You have **52 points** to distribute among:
    - Strength (STR)
    - Agility (AGI)
    - Spirit (SPI)
    - Social (SOC)
- Each attribute must be **between 0 and 20**.
- The modifier = *(Score − 10) ÷ 2*, rounded down.
- If an attribute ≤ 5 → it imposes a **permanent penalty** (determined after creation, during the first session of each adventure).

---

### **3. Derived Statistics**

Calculate the following from your attributes:

- **Hit Points** = 60 + (5 × Strength)
- **Movement** = 5 m + Agility/2
- **Initiative** = Agility − 10
- **Passive Perception** = 10 + Perception modifier
- **Tokens** = 1 + each attribute's modifier

---

### **4. Skills**

- You start with **4 skills**.
- An attribute **cannot have more skills** than its modifier.
- Proficiency in a skill = double the bonus for that skill.

---

### **5. Totem**

- Each character has a **basic Totem**.
- The Totem provides a **minor or utility effect**.
- Its appearance and purpose are defined **with the GM**.

---

### **6. Abilities**

- Abilities are created **with the GM**.
- Initially, you can know **a maximum of Spirit/2 abilities**.

---

### **7. Narrative Aspects**

- Choose a **Background**: where your character comes from and what shaped them.
- Define a **Goal**: what they want to accomplish, repair or attain.
- Describe their **Bonds with other characters**: positive relationships, tensions, alliances and distrust.
- Choose a **Free Aspect**: a distinctive trait, way of acting, belief or tendency that influences their behavior.

# **Attributes and Their Roles**

Attributes are the core of the system, defining each character's physical, mental and social abilities. They determine your effectiveness in combat, your skills, and your ability to use abilities or interact with the environment. Each attribute influences specific mechanics.

1. **Strength**:
    - **HP**: 60 + 5 * Strength.
    - Lets you wear armor and improves its damage reduction capacity.
    - **Strength Tokens:** Draw an extra card.
    - **Strength Tokens:** Repeat a basic attack.
    - Strength ability cost adjustment: Cost = Initial cost − (Strength − 10).
2. **Agility**:
    - **Movement**: 5 + Agility ÷ 2 (rounded up).
    - **Initiative**: 2 × Agility modifier.
    - **Agility Tokens**: Choose the deck to draw from.
    - **Agility Tokens**: Curve a basic attack.
    - Agility ability cost adjustment: Cost = Initial cost − (Agility − 10).
3. **Spirit**:
    - **Number of known abilities**: Spirit ÷ 2 (rounded up).
    - **Spirit Tokens:** Extend your hand size and crystallize cards.
    - **Spirit Tokens:** Make a basic attack reverberate.
    - Key statistic for magical/elemental damage.
    - Spirit ability cost adjustment: Cost = Initial cost − (Spirit − 10).
4. **Social**:
    - **Social Tokens:** Exchange cards with other players (see tokens later).
    - **Social Tokens:** Allows you to… [sentence incomplete in the source].
    - Play a central role in roleplaying interactions and social checks.
    - Social ability cost adjustment: Cost = Initial cost − (Social − 10).

## **Assigning Attributes**

- When creating your character, you have **52 points** to distribute among **Strength**, **Agility**, **Spirit** and **Social**.
- Each attribute must be between **0** and **20**.

| **Value** | **Modifier** |
| --- | --- |
| **1-9** | (Value − 10) ÷ 2, rounded down to the nearest integer (e.g. 8 → −1). |
| **10-20** | (Value − 10) ÷ 2 (e.g. 14 → +2). |

### **Rules:**

1. **Penalties:**
    - Any attribute below **6** causes **permanent penalties** (see **Attribute Penalties**).
2. **Attribute modifier:**
    - The modifier affects your rolls, including damage, skills and interactions associated with that attribute.

# **SKILLS**

Skills represent your character's specific capabilities and are central to roleplaying interactions. They resolve situations such as persuading an NPC, deciphering ancient writings or sneaking around. Each skill is linked to an attribute, reflecting your character's strengths both in and out of combat.

## **Strength**

1. **Athletics**: Run, climb, swim or lift heavy objects.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Saving throw against restraining effects. Check to push an enemy.
    
2. **Resilience**: Resist pain and poison, and endure extreme conditions.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Saving throw to maintain concentration. Check to regain consciousness.

---

## **Agility**

1. **Acrobatics**: Maintain balance and dodge nimbly.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Saving throw against area-of-effect abilities.
    
2. **Stealth**: Hide and move undetected.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Skill check to hide in combat.
    
3. **Sleight of Hand**: Steal objects, pick locks and manipulate small objects.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Used in consumable mechanics.

---

## **Spirit**

1. **Arcana**: Understand magic, rituals and enchantments.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Saving throw against targeted magical effects.
    
2. **Investigation**: Analyze clues and solve puzzles.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Skill check to analyze a creature.
    
3. **Perception**: Notice details and detect dangers.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Notice invisible enemies or traps.
    
4. **Knowledge**: Knowledge of civilizations, myths and customs.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: No direct effect, but may be useful in context.
    
5. **Survival**: Follow tracks and adapt to the environment.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: No direct effect, but may be useful in context.

---

## **Social**

1. **Persuasion**: Convince others through logical or charming arguments.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Warn an ally about an ability targeting them.
    
2. **Deception**: Lie, bluff and manipulate emotions.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Flatter an enemy into believing they are stronger than they really are.
    
3. **Intimidation**: Apply pressure through threats or force of personality.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Intimidate in reaction to an enemy attack to make the opponent hesitate.
    
4. **Performance**: Captivate through music, theater or dance.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Taunt an enemy.
    
5. **Insight**: Read others' intentions and emotions.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Resist effects that influence thought.
    
6. **Animal Handling**: Understand and train animals.
    
    ➝ **Combat**: Required to have a pet or mount.

---

## **Making a Skill Check**

1. 🎲 **Roll the Base Die**
    - Roll **1d20**.
2. ➕ **Apply Advantages**
    - For each **advantage**, add **1 extra die** to the roll (because it is a d20).
    - At the end, **keep the best result** among all the dice rolled.
3. 🧮 **Add Modifiers**
    - Add the **associated attribute modifier**.
    - If you are **proficient in the skill**, add **twice** your attribute modifier (instead of once).
4. 🧾 **Calculate the Final Total**
    - Total = **best d20 + modifier(s)**.
    - Compare this total with the **difficulty threshold (DC)** set by the GM or system.
    - Result ≥ DC → success; otherwise, failure.

## **Assigning Skills**

- Each player can choose **4 skills in total**.
- The number of skills associated with an attribute cannot exceed its **modifier**.
- **Example:** With a Spirit modifier of +3, the player can choose up to **3 Spirit skills**.
- Proficiency in a skill **doubles** your bonus in that skill.

# 🎭 **Narrative System**

---

## **1. Character Structure**

Each character has several **narrative aspects** representing their personality and experiences.

These aspects do not provide fixed bonuses; they influence rolls according to the situation.

### **General Aspects**

- **Background**: your past, profession or upbringing.
    
    *Example: former soldier, street thief, apprentice mage.*
    
- **Goal**: your personal objective or main motivation.
    
    *Example: find my sister, redeem myself, become a master craftsperson.*
    
- **Bonds**: your relationships with other characters, positive or negative.
    
    *Example: I protect Thalen at all costs; I distrust Lyra.*

---

### **Attribute Aspects**

Each attribute (Strength, Agility, Spirit, Social) has **two possible aspects**.

The player chooses one per attribute.

| Attribute | Aspect | Description | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Strength** | **Life Scar** | An ordeal, wound or memory that left its mark on you. | “I survived the mine collapse.” |
|  | **Drive** | The energy or impulse that pushes you to act. | “I refuse to give up, whatever the cost.” |
| **Agility** | **Habits & Tics** | Instinctive physical habits or reflexes. | “I always tap my foot before acting.” |
|  | **Instinct** | A quick, intuitive response, often before thought. | “I always leap before I think.” |
| **Spirit** | **Beliefs** | Your principles, faith and view of the world. | “I believe everything has a hidden reason.” |
|  | **Education** | Your learned knowledge, reasoning and cultural background. | “Trained at Escalune's engineering academy.” |
| **Social** | **Personality** | Your temperament and natural way of being. | “Always sarcastic, even when afraid.” |
|  | **Reputation** | How others see you. | “People think I am braver than I really am.” |

Aspects must be consistent with the character's attributes.

A character with low Social cannot have a perfect reputation.

This is not a numerical rule, but a **consistency requirement**.

> Strong attributes produce aspects the character has mastered.
>
> Weak attributes produce unstable or fragile aspects.

# **Attribute Penalties**

- If an attribute is **5** or less, you suffer **1 penalty for every two points below 6** (from 0 to 3 penalties).
- Penalties are determined **randomly** by rolling a **d6** at the **end of character creation**, using the lists below.

### **Important:**

- **Penalties are permanent**: increasing the attribute through items, levels or other means **does not remove these penalties**.
- However, penalties can be **removed or reduced through roleplaying events or actions**, at the GM's discretion.

## **Strength ≤ 5:**

1. **Cannot Stay Upright**
    - Whenever you take damage, make a **Strength saving throw (DC 10)**. On a failure, you fall **Prone**.
    - Standing up costs **a full action** instead of a bonus action.
    - You often fall over in everyday life.
2. **Light as a Feather**
    - You count as **light**.
    - Taking more than **5 damage from a single hit** throws you **3 meters**.
    - You can be affected by **spells or effects targeting light objects**.
    - You often lose your balance, especially when someone shouts nearby.
3. **Cannot Carry Anything**
    - You **have no inventory** and cannot carry objects, including your own weapon.
    - You must retrieve your weapon at the start of combat (cost: **1 bonus action**).
4. **Cannot Equip Anything**
    - You cannot carry or equip a **weapon**, **armor**, **Focus** or **shield**.
    - You wear only very light clothes.
5. **Physical Dependence**
    - You cannot carry objects weighing more than **5 kg**.
    - You need help to **operate heavy mechanisms**, such as doors or levers.
6. **Vulnerable**
    - If you fall unconscious, you cannot recover on your own.
    - If someone helps you recover, you remain **inert** (you skip your turn) and **vulnerable** (you can take additional damage).
    - You need a nap once a day.

## **Agility ≤ 5:**

1. **Physical Disadvantage:**
    - Disadvantage on all rolls involving a physical action (including attacks).
2. **No Reactions or Bonus Actions:**
    - You cannot use **reactions** or **bonus actions**.
    - **Reaction Totem** prohibited.
    - You cannot react quickly to events around you.
3. **Total Vulnerability:**
    - You cannot make **any saving throw** against attacks or effects that require dodging.
    - Enemies **cannot miss you**, even on a **natural 1**.
4. **Risk of Injury:**
    - Any failed physical action causes **1d4 damage**.
5. **Hidden Cards:**
    - You cannot look at your cards before playing them.
    - When you acquire an object, there is a chance you will break it.
6. **Limited Movement:**
    - You can move only during the **first 2 turns of combat**.
    - After that, you must use the **Dash** action to move.
    - You cannot walk for long and need help to climb anything.

## **Spirit ≤ 5:**

1. **Magical and Technical Inability**
    - You cannot use abilities.
    - You need help to understand or use any **complex mechanism** (tools, locked doors, puzzles, etc.).
2. **Foolish and Unpredictable**
    - **Disabled Totem:** Your totem's effect is canceled.
    - **Random Decisions:** When a critical decision arises, the GM may roll a die to decide for you.
3. **Fear of Magic**
    - **Fear of Powerful Spells:**
        - Whenever a spell costing more than 16 is cast within 10 m, make a Spirit check (DC 10).
        - On a failure, you are **Frightened** for 1 turn.
    - **Risks from Powerful Objects:**
        - If you interact with a divine or powerful object, make a Spirit check (DC 10).
        - On a failure, you suffer an **affliction** chosen by the GM (curse, rejection, etc.).
4. **Hostility to Magic**
    - **No Magical Weapons:** You cannot carry or use magical weapons or artifacts.
    - **Clumsy Combat Rituals:**
        - You must draw your weapon at the start of combat.
        - While your weapon is drawn, you cannot speak except to **hurl insults**.
    - **No Professional Skill:** You have mastered **no trade**.
5. **Illiterate and Lost**
    - **Complete Illiteracy:**
        - You cannot read, write or count.
        - You do not understand money: all prices are random and often higher.
    - **Random Consumption:** You cannot choose a consumable; you always consume a random item.
    - **Random Starting Equipment:** If you have several weapons, you start combat with a randomly selected weapon.
6. **Passive Anti-Magic**
    - **Immunity to Buffs:** You cannot receive any positive magical effect or buff.
    - **Inability to Detect:** You cannot detect **hidden or concealed** enemies, even if an ally warns you.

## **Social ≤ 5:**

1. **Universal Hostility**
    - **Attempts to negotiate prices** automatically fail for you and your companions.
    - When you are attacked, make a **Social saving throw** (DC 10); on a failure, sheathe your weapon.
2. **Social and Physical Repulsion**
    - You have disadvantage on **Stealth** checks.
    - You take **1d4 psychic damage** per person in your zone of control.
    - Non-hostile creatures (such as animals) avoid you or react aggressively.
3. **Silence in Combat**
    - Any attempt at persuasion sounds like deception; you always seem to be lying.
    - You cannot lie; any attempt at deception automatically fails.
    - You speak so softly that nobody can hear you beyond **3 meters**.
4. **Unsettling Presence**
    - Disadvantage on **all your rolls** if more than **3 people** are within 10 meters of you.
    - You give your companions **disadvantage on Persuasion checks**.
    - People completely forget you exist **2 hours** after meeting you.
5. **Tourette Syndrome**
    - Whenever you speak, there is a chance you will involuntarily blurt out an insult.
    - You cannot cooperate to use **Social tokens** (card exchanges).
6. **Guilty and Clumsy**
    - You do not speak the language, and there is a **1 in 2 chance** that your words will be misinterpreted.
    - You **cannot target your allies with abilities** or be targeted by them.
