## 🧠 System

- **d20s** are used for **checks and saving throws**.
- **d4 to d12 dice** are used for **attacks, values and bonuses**.
- **Advantage**: add **+2 dice**, then keep the **best results**.

**Hand of cards:**

- You have 3 **cards in hand** (you draw **+1 card per round**).
- **Crystallized cards (red)** do not count towards your **hand** (except **Totem**).
- **Start of turn:**
    - discard cards beyond your limit,
    - then **draw 1 card**.

**Tokens (recovered during a short rest):**

- 💪 **Strength** → **draw** \ chain actions
- 🏃 **Agility** → **exchange** a card for a specific suit \ multiply an AOE ×3
- 🔮 **Spirit** → **crystallize** cards \ magical attack reverberation
- 🎭 **Social** → **exchange** cards with **allies** \ have an ally attack

---

## ✨ Aspects

- **Invoking an aspect** lets you add the value of the spent card to the roll, as justified by the narrative.
- Using an aspect **against yourself** lets you draw a **Crystallized Card**, but causes a **narrative complication** handled by the GM.

---

## ⚔️ Narrative combat

- A **round** has two phases: the **GM phase**, then the **player phase**.
- Each character can perform:
    - **1 Action (initiative roll)**
    - **1 Bonus Action (initiative roll)**
    - **1 Reaction (no initiative)**

**Standard effects** last **1 round**, unless otherwise stated.

---

## 🎲 Rolls

**Skill check**

- Roll **d20 + modifier**.
- The result must be **≥ the difficulty (DC)** set by the GM.
- **Proficiency** doubles the modifier applied.

**Attack**

- **Weapon die + modifier + bonus + context**.

**Advantage**

- Add **+2 dice**, then keep the best results.
- **Finesse** weapons use **Agility**.

**Critical**

- If a die rolls its **maximum**, reroll it and **add the result**.
- Criticals can **chain** if the rerolls also produce the maximum.

**Failure**

- A **natural 1** is a **miss**.

---

## 🛡️ Defense

- **Armor**: reduces damage by **up to a maximum of 50%**.

**Parry (Reaction)**

- Reduces damage by a **flat amount before armor**.
- Approximate value:

    **(maximum weapon die value/2 + Strength or Agility Mod + armor Deflection)**.

---

## 💀 Effects and conditions

- The **standard duration** of an effect is **1 round**.

**Saving throws:**

- 💪 **Strength (Athletics)** → physical effects
- 🏃 **Agility (Acrobatics)** → area effects (AOE)
- 🔮 **Spirit (Arcana)** → magical effects
- 🎭 **Social (Insight)** → mental effects

**Legendary Break**

- **Bosses** become **free after 2 turns** of an effect.
